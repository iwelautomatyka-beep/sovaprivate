Sova Nanny – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026 · Applies to the Android app “Sova Nanny” (package com.iwelautomatyka.sova)

The short version. Sova Nanny turns two Android phones into a baby monitor over your own Wi-Fi. There is no account, no sign-in, no server of ours and no cloud. Sound and video travel directly from the phone in the nursery to the phone in your pocket and never leave your home network. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data, and the app contains no advertising or analytics.

Who we are

Sova Nanny is published by ForgeLabs Systems (Poland). Questions about this policy: contact@forgelabs.systems.

What the app does with sound and video

  • Microphone (nursery phone). The phone placed in the nursery listens continuously while monitoring is on. The audio is analysed on that phone to tell crying from other sounds, and – depending on the listening mode you choose – streamed to the parent phone over your local Wi-Fi network. Audio is not recorded, not saved and not uploaded. When monitoring is stopped, the microphone is released.
  • Microphone (parent phone). Used only while you hold the “Talk” button, to send your voice to the nursery phone. Nothing is stored.
  • Camera (nursery phone, optional). Turned on only while a parent phone is actively watching, and only if you allowed camera access. The video is streamed to the parent phone over the local network and is never recorded or uploaded.
  • Sound classification. The cry detector runs entirely on the nursery phone using an on-device model. No audio or derived data is sent to us or to any third party.

Data we collect

None. We have no servers, no user accounts and no analytics or crash-reporting libraries in the app. We cannot see whether you use the app, how, or what it hears.

The app keeps a small amount of information on your phone only, so it can work: the role of the phone (nursery or parent), a four-digit pairing code, the local network address and name of the paired phone, your alert and sound settings, and a short diagnostic log kept in memory for troubleshooting (it is not written to a file we can access and disappears when the app is closed). This information stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Network use

The two phones talk to each other directly over the Wi-Fi network they share. The app uses local network discovery (mDNS and a UDP beacon) so the parent phone can find the nursery phone, and a direct TCP connection between the two phones for audio, video, status and commands. The app does not connect to the internet; if the phones are not on the same network, it does not work at all.

Children

The app is intended for parents and caregivers. The sound it processes may include a child’s voice, but it is processed only on your own devices, is never recorded, and is never transmitted outside your home network. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The app includes no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting components. The on-device sound-classification library (LiteRT) runs locally and does not communicate over the network. The app is distributed through Google Play; Google’s own data practices for app distribution are described in Google’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Live video is an optional one-time purchase (“Sova Pro”) made through Google Play. The payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. The app only stores on the phone the fact that the purchase was made, so the feature stays unlocked. A nursery phone remembers that a parent phone with Sova Pro has connected to it, so the whole family can use video after one purchase. No purchase information leaves your devices other than through Google Play itself.

Security

A parent phone can connect to a nursery phone only with the four-digit pairing code shown on the nursery phone. Traffic stays within your local network; its security depends on the security of your Wi-Fi network, so we recommend a password-protected network you control.

Your choices

  • Stop monitoring at any time from the app or from the notification; this releases the microphone and camera.
  • Revoke any permission in Android settings; the related feature stops working, the rest of the app continues.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all its data from the phone.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date changed. The current version is always available at this address.
