The short version

Sova Nanny turns two Android phones into a baby monitor. There is no account and nothing to sign in to. At home, sound and video go straight from the phone in the nursery to the phone in your pocket, over your own Wi-Fi. Two things use the internet, and both pass through a server of ours: alerts, so that the baby can wake you when you are out, and, with Sova Pro, listening and watching while you are out. Both are encrypted with a key we never receive, so we cannot hear or see anything, and nothing is recorded anywhere. There is no advertising, no analytics and nothing to sell.

What leaves your phones

At home, nothing. Sound, video, your voice and every command travel straight between the two phones over the Wi-Fi they share. They do not pass through us and they are not recorded.

Away from home, only these four things leave, and nothing else:

  • Alerts. The nursery phone tells our server that crying started or stopped, that the phone is overheating, and that it is still alive, together with its battery level, temperature and signal strength. No sound and no video are ever part of this. Crying is recognised on the nursery phone itself; only the answer travels.
  • Sound and video, with Sova Pro. While you listen or watch from outside your home, the nursery phone sends them to your phone through our server, and your voice goes back the same way. We pass the bytes on and keep none of them. They are encrypted with a key only your two phones can work out, so we cannot hear or see anything, and neither can anyone who gets hold of our server. The same connection is opened for a few seconds in two other moments: when you ask for the free twenty minutes, because that request has to reach the nursery phone, and just after you finish speaking, so that a second message starts at once instead of waiting. In both cases it carries nothing but that request, and closes by itself.
  • A key that proves it is you. Your two phones share a secret when you pair them, and that secret never leaves them. What our server receives is a different key made from it, enough to check that messages really come from your phones and useless for anything else.
  • An address for notifications. A token from Google that lets us wake your phone. It is the only way to reach an Android phone with a notification while the app is not running.

All of this is switched on by default and can be switched off on the nursery phone. With it off, the app never contacts a server of ours at all; listening and watching from outside stop working too.

What we keep, and for how long

For each pair of phones our server keeps the key that checks your signatures, the notification addresses of your phones, and the last thing the nursery reported: battery, temperature and whether crying is in progress. Alerts are passed on and not stored. Sound and video are never stored, anywhere. When the nursery phone goes quiet for several minutes, we tell your phone that it is offline. When it has been quiet for about a month, everything we hold for those phones is deleted by itself.

Our server's own diagnostic logs are kept deliberately blind: they record the room's random name, the server's own decisions and timings, and nothing from your home. Not the battery level, not the temperature, and not whether the baby was crying.

We have no user accounts and no list of our users. The server runs on Cloudflare and notifications are delivered by Google; both handle only what is described here. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in the app, and we do not sell anything to anyone.

What stays on your phone

So that it can work, the app keeps on the phone itself: whether this phone is the nursery or the parent, the four-digit pairing code, the shared secret, the address and name of the paired phone, your sound and alert settings, and a short diagnostic log that lives in memory and disappears when the app closes. None of this reaches us. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes all of it.

Microphone and camera

  • Nursery phone. It listens the whole time monitoring is on, so that it can tell crying from other sounds. Audio is not recorded and not saved. When you stop monitoring, the microphone is released.
  • Parent phone. The microphone is used only while you hold the Talk button, to send your voice to the nursery. Nothing is stored.
  • Camera. Switched on only while a parent is actually watching, and only if you allowed camera access. The picture is never recorded.

Permissions

Android shows you the full list before you grant anything. The ones worth explaining:

Notifications
The alarm, and the quiet notice that tells you monitoring is running.

Children

The app is for parents and carers. The sound it listens to will include a child's voice, but that sound is examined on your own phone and never recorded. When you are away from home, what reaches us is the verdict, "crying", and never the sound itself; with Sova Pro the sound passes through us encrypted with a key we do not have. We collect no personal information about anyone, children included.

Purchases

Live video, and listening and watching while away from home, are a one-time purchase called Sova Pro, made through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The phone remembers that the purchase was made, and the nursery remembers that a parent with Sova Pro has connected to it, so one purchase covers the whole family. You can try these features for twenty minutes before buying. The nursery phone counts those minutes and only while something is actually playing; if you ask for them while away from home, the request travels to it through our server like everything else described above.

Security

A parent phone joins the nursery by scanning the code on its screen or typing its four-digit PIN. The PIN itself is never sent over the network, and the nursery blocks a device that keeps guessing. Everything the two phones say to each other afterwards is encrypted, so neither your neighbours nor we can read it. Giving the room a new code on the nursery phone disconnects every paired phone and makes the old one unreachable.

If you want to know exactly how that is done, down to the names of the algorithms, it is all written out in How Sova is secured.

Your choices

  • Stop monitoring at any time, from the app or from its notification. The microphone and camera are released at once.
  • Switch alerts over the internet off on the nursery phone, and nothing ever leaves your home again.
  • Close the listening or video screen, and the stream through our server stops at once; the connection itself closes a few seconds later. Nothing of it remains anywhere.
  • Use "Forget nursery" on a parent phone to take that phone out of the pair and off our server.
  • Uninstall the app, and everything it kept on the phone goes with it.
