The short version

Sova Nanny turns two Android phones into a baby monitor. There is no account and no sign-in. At home, sound and video travel directly from the phone in the nursery to the phone in your pocket over your own Wi-Fi and never leave your home network. Two things use the internet, and both go through a server of ours: alerts over the internet (on by default, with a switch on the nursery phone) send short event messages (“crying”, “overheating”, “still alive”) so the parent phone can be woken even away from home; and listening and watching away from home (part of the optional Sova Pro purchase) relays the nursery’s sound and video, encrypted, to your phone while you are listening or watching. The server forwards and stores nothing of what it relays, and no sound or video is ever recorded anywhere. We do not sell data and the app contains no advertising or analytics.

What the app does with sound and video

  • Microphone (nursery phone). The phone placed in the nursery listens continuously while monitoring is on. The audio is analysed on that phone to tell crying from other sounds, and — depending on the listening mode you choose — streamed to the parent phone over your local Wi-Fi network, or, when the parent phone is away from home and has Sova Pro, through our relay server (see Alerts and relay over the internet). Audio is not recorded and not saved, on the phones or on the server. When monitoring is stopped, the microphone is released.
  • Microphone (parent phone). Used only while you hold the “Talk” button, to send your voice to the nursery phone. Nothing is stored.
  • Camera (nursery phone, optional). Turned on only while a parent phone is actively watching, and only if you allowed camera access. The video is streamed to the parent phone over the local network, or through our relay server when the parent phone is away from home with Sova Pro, and is never recorded, on the phones or on the server.
  • Sound classification. The cry detector runs entirely on the nursery phone using an on-device model. No audio or derived data is sent to us or to any third party.

Data we collect

Nothing beyond the alert messages and the relayed stream described below, and nothing at all if you switch “Alerts over the internet” off. There are no user accounts and no analytics or crash-reporting libraries in the app. With that feature off, the app never contacts a server of ours (listening and watching away from home need it too, so they are off as well) and we cannot see whether you use the app, how, or what it hears.

The app keeps a small amount of information on your phone only, so it can work: the role of the phone (nursery or parent), a four-digit pairing code, a random room key, the local network address and name of the paired phone, your alert and sound settings, and a short diagnostic log kept in memory for troubleshooting (it is not written to a file we can access and disappears when the app is closed). This information stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Alerts and relay over the internet

With Alerts over the internet on (the default, with a switch on the nursery phone), the nursery phone sends small messages to our server, and the parent phone registers with that server to receive push notifications. With Sova Pro, the same server also relays sound and video to a parent phone that is away from home, for as long as that parent is listening or watching. This is what leaves your home, and nothing else:

  • Events from the nursery phone: that crying or shouting was detected and when it stopped, that the phone is overheating, and a “still alive” heartbeat (every five minutes while every parent phone is at home, every minute while one is away) with the phone’s battery level, charging state, temperature, signal strength, uptime, whether crying is in progress, when the last cry started and ended, whether Sova Pro or its trial is active for this room, the time of the last system alert, and the nursery phone’s own push token (so the server can wake it when a parent wants to listen). No sound, no video, no transcript of any kind is ever sent — the cry detector runs on the nursery phone and only its verdict travels.
  • A room key: a random 128-bit secret generated on the nursery phone and handed to the parent phone at pairing, encrypted (see Security). The server stores it so it can verify that messages come from your phones. The server identifies your room by a hash of this key; the key is not your name, your Wi-Fi name or any identifier of you.
  • Listening and watching away from home (Sova Pro): when you turn on Listen or Watch while your phone is not on the nursery’s Wi-Fi, the nursery phone sends compressed audio (and video, if you asked for it) to the parent phone through our server, and your voice travels the same way back when you hold Talk. The server passes the bytes on and keeps nothing: each message is held in memory only for the moment of forwarding, never written to disk, and the stream stops the instant you close the screen or switch listening off. The bytes are encrypted between the two phones with keys derived from the room key (AES-256-GCM, a fresh key per session). Because the server also holds the room key to verify that messages come from your phones, this encryption is a guarantee against everyone on the path except the server itself; what the server does with the stream is exactly and only what this paragraph says. Nothing is recorded.
  • From the parent phone: a push-notification token issued by Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging). It is a device identifier that lets the server ask Google to wake your phone; it is the only way an Android phone can be reached with a notification while the app is not running.

The server keeps, per room: the room key, the push tokens of registered parent phones and of the nursery phone, the time and content of the last heartbeat (battery, temperature, crying state, Pro or trial state), whether the nursery reported itself switched off, and the timing it needs to tell whether the nursery is still alive. Events are forwarded immediately and not stored; relayed sound and video are not stored at all. If the server does not hear from a nursery phone for about twelve minutes (about two and a half minutes while a parent is away from home) it sends a “nursery offline” notification to the parent phones. A room whose nursery phone has been silent for thirty days is deleted automatically. The server is hosted on Cloudflare Workers; notifications are delivered through Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging. Both act as our service providers and receive only what is described here. All traffic uses TLS and every message is signed with the room key.

Switching the feature off on the nursery phone stops all messages immediately. “Forget nursery” on a parent phone removes its push token from the room. Generating a new pairing code on the nursery phone creates a new room key, which makes the old room unreachable.

Network use

The two phones talk to each other directly over the Wi-Fi network they share. The app uses local network discovery (mDNS and a UDP beacon) so the parent phone can find the nursery phone, and a direct, encrypted TCP connection between the two phones for audio, video, status and commands. Without that shared network, sound and video work only away from home with Sova Pro, through our relay. The only internet traffic is the alert and relay traffic described above, plus Google Play for purchases.

Children

The app is intended for parents and caregivers. The sound it processes may include a child’s voice, but it is analysed only on your own devices and is never recorded. With alerts over the internet on, what leaves your home is the detector’s verdict (“crying detected”), never the sound itself; with Sova Pro, while you listen or watch away from home, the sound and video pass through our server encrypted and are forwarded without being stored. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The app includes no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting components. The on-device sound-classification library (LiteRT) runs locally and does not communicate over the network. For the optional alerts over the internet we use two service providers: Cloudflare (hosting of the alert server) and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivery of push notifications; the Firebase library in the app is used for this purpose only and its analytics are disabled). The app is distributed through Google Play; Google’s own data practices for app distribution are described in Google’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Live video, and listening and watching away from home, are an optional one-time purchase (“Sova Pro”) made through Google Play. The payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. The app only stores on the phone the fact that the purchase was made, so the feature stays unlocked. A nursery phone remembers that a parent phone with Sova Pro has connected to it, so the whole family can use these features after one purchase. Before buying you can try them for twenty minutes; the nursery phone counts that time and the parent phone shows how much is left. Whether Pro or the trial is active for a room is included in the nursery’s heartbeat to our server, so the server knows whether to relay; no purchase information leaves your devices other than that flag and Google Play itself.

Security

A parent phone joins a nursery phone by scanning the code on its screen or by typing its four-digit PIN. The PIN itself is never sent over the network: pairing uses a password-authenticated key exchange (SRP-6a), so a device on your Wi-Fi that watches the exchange learns nothing, and guessing requires a separate attempt per guess, which the nursery phone limits (five wrong PINs lock pairing for a minute; twenty replace the PIN). After pairing, every connection between the two phones is authenticated with the room key and encrypted end to end (ephemeral ECDH P-256, AES-256-GCM), so sound, video and commands cannot be read or injected by other devices on the same network. Alert traffic to our server is encrypted (TLS) and every message is signed with the room key; the relayed stream is additionally encrypted between the phones as described above. Giving the room a new code on the nursery phone replaces both the PIN and the room key and disconnects every paired phone.

Your choices

  • Stop monitoring at any time from the app or from the notification; this releases the microphone and camera.
  • Revoke any permission in Android settings; the related feature stops working, the rest of the app continues.
  • Switch “Alerts over the internet” off on the nursery phone and nothing ever leaves your home; the messages stop immediately, and listening and watching away from home stop working.
  • Close the listening or video screen, or switch listening off, and the relay stops at once; nothing of it remains anywhere.
  • “Forget nursery” on a parent phone removes that phone from the room on our server. A room is deleted automatically after thirty days without contact.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all its data from the phone.
